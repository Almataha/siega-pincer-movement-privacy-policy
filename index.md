# Privacy Policy

Welcome to **Siega - Pincer Movement** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you download, access, or play **Siega - Pincer Movement** (the "Game").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or playing the Game, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you play **Siega - Pincer Movement**, we collect information to provide game functionality, facilitate authentication, analyze installation attribution, and deliver targeted advertising.

### A. Information Provided via Authentication Services
We offer social and third-party sign-in options managed via **Google Firebase Authentication**. Depending on the login method you choose, we collect the following:

* **Facebook Login:** We request access only to your **Public Profile** (your public name, profile picture, and scoped Facebook User ID) and your **Facebook Friends List** (specifically friends who also play this Game)..
 * **Guest / Anonymous Login:** A temporary, randomly generated Firebase User ID to maintain save data without personal identity.

### B. Automatically Collected Technical & Analytical Data
Certain data is collected automatically when you launch and play the Game via integrated Third-Party Software Development Kits (SDKs) including Unity Engine, Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, and AppLovin MAX:

* **Device & Hardware Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, system language, and unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android).
* **Network & Approximate Location:** IP address and general geographic location (country or city level).
* **Attribution & Deep Linking Data:** Click data, referrer URLs, install source, and deferred deep-linking data processed via **AppsFlyer OneLink**.
* **Game Performance & Diagnostics:** Crash reports, loading speed, session duration, and gameplay telemetry.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for operational, analytical, and commercial purposes related to the Game:

* **Core Gameplay & Cloud Saves:** To create your player account, sync save states across devices, and manage player progression via **Firebase**.
* **Social & Leaderboard Features:** To display in-game leaderboards, connect social interactions, and list your Facebook friends who also play the Game.
* **Attribution & Campaign Measurement:** Measure  marketing performance, track install sources, and route players to specific in-game content via **AppsFlyer OneLink**.
* **Advertising & Monetization:** Display contextual and personalized advertisements via **AppLovin MAX** and its ad network partners.
* **Game Optimization:** Identify bugs, optimize performance, and evaluate feature updates.
* **Compliance:** Ensure adherence to Google Play, Apple App Store, and Meta developer policies.

## 4. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to maintain your game account and satisfy legal obligations. You have complete control over your personal data and can request deletion at any time regardless of the authentication method used (**Guest, Facebook, Google, or Apple**).

### A. How to Delete Your Data In-App
1. Launch **Siega - Pincer Movement**.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon on the main menu screen.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the prompt.

* **What Gets Deleted:** Executing this action permanently deletes your player account, cloud saves, level progression, inventory, and linked Firebase User ID from our servers.
* **Third-Party Auth Tokens:** Deleting your in-game account does not modify your primary Facebook account. You can revoke this Game's permissions anytime inside your Facebook Security Settings.

### B. Manual Deletion Requests
If you cannot access the Game or have uninstalled it from your device, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing us at **contact@almataha.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*.
